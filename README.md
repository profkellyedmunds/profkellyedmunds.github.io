# Prof Kelly Edmunds – personal website

The personal academic website of Prof Kelly Edmunds (PhD · NTF · CATE · SFHEA), Professor of Biology Education and Student Experience and Associate Dean Student Experience and Outcomes, Faculty of Science, University of East Anglia.

Kelly helps universities improve student belonging, engagement and success through research, strategy and evidence-informed practice.

## What's on the site

### About
An introduction to Kelly's work on the undergraduate and postgraduate student experience, with a focus on belonging, engagement, continuation, success and progression. Includes her research interests (student belonging, transitions into HE, postgraduate experience, practical skills and assessment), how she can help institutions, and an at-a-glance summary of her current role, recognition, the Student Belonging Community of Practice (600+ members), her book and her role with the Zoological Society of East Anglia.

### Scholarship
Research and enhancement projects, including the multi-institutional PGT student experience project, Objective Structured Practical Evaluations, student belonging research, the pre-arrival platform, vivas as assessment, and the Voices of Belonging podcast. Also lists her leadership roles, the courses and modules she has led, and her teaching.

### Awards
Her 2025 National Teaching Fellowship and Collaborative Award for Teaching Excellence (Advance HE), other awards and nominations, and funding received.

### Publications
Peer-reviewed articles, books, book chapters, preprints and work in progress, with search and filters by type. The list updates itself from OpenAlex using Kelly's ORCID (0000-0001-5351-3721), so new papers appear automatically.

### Writing
The co-edited book *Student Belonging in Action* (Routledge, 2025), her contributions to the Oxford University Press textbook *Biological Sciences: Exploring the Science of Life*, and commentary and blog pieces for THE Campus, Wonkhe, SEDA and the Centre for Belonging in Education.

### Talks
Keynotes, invited talks and panels in the UK, Europe and the US, with earlier talks in a collapsible list.

### Engagement
Public engagement, including Animal Dissection Live at the Royal Institution, Norwich Science Festival, BBC Radio 4, Latitude Festival and the UEA Children's Christmas Lecture.

### Career
Appointments, qualifications, professional recognition, and external and institutional roles.

Email, ORCID and LinkedIn.
